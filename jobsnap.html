● Project Overview & Marketplace Workflow

Traditional freelance service platforms suffer from high latency in contractor response, complicated checkout workflows, and inadequate real-time communication between customers and local service providers.

The Solution: Jobsnap streamlined the on-demand task hiring lifecycle. Customers publish local or remote tasks, and verified service providers place instant proposals with competitive bids. Once accepted, bidirectional WebSocket communication provides end-to-end milestone coordination.

● Technical Architecture & Key Features

3-Tier Multi-Role Structure: Dedicated user interfaces and permissions for Users, Service Providers, and Business Agents.
WebSocket Chat & Bidding: Sub-second message delivery, live proposal negotiation, and push sync.
Profile Verification & KYC: Identity verification and credential uploads for service professionals.
Dynamic Milestone Tracking: Real-time task progress statuses from initiation to escrow completion.
