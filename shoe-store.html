A high-converting sneaker shopping mobile application connecting an image-led visual catalog with multi-region size selection, smart search discovery, shopping bag management, and live delivery milestone tracking.

● The Challenge & Solution

Sneaker shoppers need to move rapidly from visual inspiration to selecting a specific colorway and regional size standard (US/UK/EU). After checkout, customers expect immediate clarity on where their shipment stands without digging through external carrier links.

The Solution: SoleStep leads with bold sneaker cards that transition smoothly into a detail screen grouping colorway swatches, sizing systems, and one-tap bag actions. The integrated account hub connects order history directly to a step-by-step carrier delivery timeline.

● The Shopping Experience & Technical Highlights

Browse & Smart Search: Category chips, trending search tags, and recent query history accelerate sneaker discovery.
Colorway & Size Matrix: Detail view groups color variants, sizing-system toggles, stock indicators, and instant Add-to-Bag actions.
Shopping Bag & Promo Engine: Real-time quantity controls, promo-code validation, shipping fee calculation, and itemized totals.
Live Delivery Timeline: Visual shipment tracker displaying estimated delivery window, carrier details, and milestone progression.
