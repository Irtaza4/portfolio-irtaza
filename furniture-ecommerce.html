● The Challenge & Solution

Furniture shopping involves more than choosing a single SKU. Shoppers need to compare upholstery finishes, understand dynamic price adjustments across materials, and review delivery logistics before placing an order — all without feeling overwhelmed on a mobile screen.

The Solution: Forma Living gives high-resolution product imagery room to lead, then groups fabric options, color swatches, quantity controls, and real-time price adjustments around the purchase decision. A structured 4-stage checkout cleanly separates address, shipping method, payment, and final order review.

● The Shopping Experience & Key Features

Visual Catalog & Discovery: Category navigation, smart search, and multi-attribute filters provide intuitive entry points for exploring collections.
Fabric & Swatch Customizer: Interactive product screen placing upholstery fabric options and color swatches beside live price updates.
Favorites & Cart Management: Saved wishlists preserve items for later, while the cart breaks down quantities, unit prices, and subtotal summaries.
4-Stage Guided Checkout: Step-by-step progression through Address, Shipping, Payment, and Overview leading to an instant order confirmation.
