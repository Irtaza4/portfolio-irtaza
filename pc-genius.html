● Project Overview & Solution

Troubleshooting PC hardware failures or selecting compatible computer parts often overwhelms everyday users due to complex technical specifications and lack of structured diagnostic guidance.

The Solution: PcGenius breaks down hardware troubleshooting into intuitive interactive decision trees, provides automated part compatibility verifications, and gives users tailored component recommendations for their budget and workload.

● Technical Highlights & Features

Guided Diagnostic Engine: Step-by-step troubleshooting wizards for CPU, GPU, RAM, and motherboard faults.
Component Compatibility Logic: Automatic socket, power wattage, and form factor clearance checks.
Custom PC Build Selector: Interactive tier builder matching gaming, creator, and workstation specs.
High-Performance Flutter UI: Clean hierarchical navigation and dynamic spec comparisons.
