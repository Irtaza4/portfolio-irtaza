● Game Overview & Mechanics

Rung King brings the authentic experience of the traditional South Asian card game Rung (Court Piece) to mobile screens. Played in a 2v2 team format, players strategically call the trump suit (Rung) and compete to win seven tricks in a round.

The Solution: Leveraging the Flame Engine within Flutter, Rung King delivers high-performance 2D card rendering, custom dealing animations, seamless WebSocket synchronizations for live 4-player lobbies, and an intelligent offline AI engine.

● Technical Highlights & Game Architecture

Flame Engine: High-performance 2D game loops, sprite management, and smooth card physics.
Low-Latency WebSockets: Synchronous game state distribution across 4 concurrent online players.
Rule Engine & AI: Custom Dart algorithms managing trump priority, trick evaluation, and heuristic offline bot moves.
Coin Stake Economy: Persistent player coin balances with per-match stake tables and win payouts.
