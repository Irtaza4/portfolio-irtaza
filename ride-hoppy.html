● Project Overview & Solution

Urban commuting generates significant personal expense and environmental impact. RideHoppy connects passengers with corporate drivers traveling along identical corridors, offering a reliable, verified, and community-driven transportation alternative.

The Solution: Built from the ground up using Flutter, RideHoppy provides seamless driver-rider matching, live map tracking, dynamic route navigation, and integrated communication tools that keep commuters safe and on schedule.

● Technical Architecture & Key Features

Smart Driver Matching: High-precision geofencing and proximity algorithms match passengers along driver corridors.
Real-Time Map Telemetry: Smooth vehicle animation markers moving on Google Maps at 60 FPS.
Corporate Verification: Organization-specific email verification for safety and security.
Digital Wallet & Tipping: In-app digital payment gateway for optional driver appreciation.
